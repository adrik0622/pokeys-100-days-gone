DEVELOPMENT COLLECTION - NOT V1.0

This collection currently contains ZombieBuddy, Project Viewpoint and the Viewpoint 3D model pack only. Bandits and Horde Nights are separate trial candidates, NOT collection members. The original Pokeys100DaysGone companion is local development source 0.3.0, not a Workshop download. Subscribe All does not install the full intended campaign.

PROGRESS
Single-player launch/movement/interaction and local vanilla two-client movement, item transfer, reconnect and clean server restart passed. Separate trials demonstrated persistent faction standing/favors/aid, calibration, debug-horde faction disruption/recovery and one armed hostile human. A later combined dedicated trial passed first-person join, faction favor/aid, 18.83-second calibration and clean-restart persistence. Full combat/horde-loop acceptance remains open.

KNOWN BUGS / LIMITATIONS - PINNED, NOT AN IMMEDIATE STOP TO DEVELOPMENT PLAY
- Viewpoint dedicated shotgun shots logged PlayerHitSquare inconsistencies. Cause unresolved; reliable zombie/NPC/player damage and obstruction are not verified.
- Bandits repeatedly logged Expected a table in its server command handler during a hostile encounter. Visible attacks do not certify synchronization/damage.
- Advance horde direction is unavailable until event start. Current-event/loaded-only accounting is not a strict global survivor cap; repeated-event accumulation remains possible.
- If a target group disconnects while others remain online, horde births can continue at its saved target. Empty-server pause alone does not verify offline-base safety.
- Clean restart persistence passed; hard-crash inventory/campaign atomicity is unverified. Keep backups; do not blindly replay pending rewards.
- Local rendered clients use substantial RAM. Steam/WAN, other platforms and 4-8-player capacity remain unverified.
- Workshop cover upload failed; cosmetic only.

NOT IMPLEMENTED / STILL TO ACCEPT
Complete recurring encounters and integrated combat/recovery; normal horde trigger, repeated cycles and mid-event restarts; day-100 survival balance and friend installation/playtest. Known issues are not fixed by documenting them.

APPROVED SCOPE CHANGE (SC-001)
The original v1.0 ask included a recognizable leader whose death stops its coordination. That full feature is now a v1.1 target, NOT mandatory for v1.0. A simpler Screamer-based threat was considered optional; the downloaded candidate's lifecycle audit did not meet our low-cost integration criteria, so it remains excluded. No replacement custom AI or second director is being added for v1.0. The core forecast/horde/faction-aftermath/recovery loop remains.

INSTALLATION
