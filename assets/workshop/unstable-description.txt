Use a fresh disposable world, matching 42.21 client/server revisions, and the exact Mod IDs/settings in our guide:
https://github.com/adrik0622/pokeys-100-days-gone/blob/unstable/docs/UNSTABLE_FIELD_GUIDE.md
ZombieBuddy requires the author's one-time loader installation; subscribing alone is insufficient. The collection does not automatically enable/configure a server. Our unpublished Pokeys companion is NOT included; its custom relay/repair/calibration bridge is a separate development feature.

[h1]Experimental combinations[/h1]
Author-documented multiplayer/build support and inspected source guide inclusion; the combined stack is being field-tested, not certified. Back up saves and expect changes. Viewpoint/CVFE/rarity combat balance, NPC ownership, Bloated visual sync and remote group performance need play feedback. No guaranteed boss relic, faction loot pursuit or automatic Jeem-to-military trust conversion is supplied.

A-Life is the sole NPC stack: do not co-enable Bandits. Disable all Horde Nights supply/helicopter/Bandits drops so Military Drop owns supply fulfillment. Do not stack other VFE packs, rarity frameworks, RV systems or overlapping pool mods. RV interiors are not included yet.

Consequence means readable choices and responses, not universal punishment for player killing. Warning, agency and recovery should accompany danger; social games are optional and chips have no survival-item redemption. Public collection, separate hidden baseline.
