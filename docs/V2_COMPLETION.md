## Human decisions that matter before calling V2 finished

- **HR-001/002:** contextual faction values/violent choices and what identity survives character death. These are product decisions, not permission for universal anti-PvP penalties.
- **HR-011:** whether locally pinned Military Drop remains acceptable while public Workshop availability is uncertain, or whether to choose a different supply-fulfillment approach. No upstream code may be mirrored/rehosted by assumption.
- **HR-010:** whether ambiguous partial rewards stay manual-inspection-only or need a deliberately constrained admin reconciliation flow. Existing latches remain untouched until decided.
- **HR-013:** sufficient resources for simultaneous modded-client tests. No second human tester is requested; local renderer memory is not a server-player limit.

RV interiors, hotwiring policy, V3 relic/guardian choices and the casino are **optional/deferred**, not extra V2 gates. The full queue retains their details without treating them as immediate development blockers.
